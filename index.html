THE GAP

Property decisions are
fragmented by default.

A listing starts the process, but it does not answer the investment question. The evidence needed to decide is spread across portals, spreadsheets, public datasets, policy updates and legal documents.

01

Listings lack investment context

Price and photos do not reveal true cash required, after-tax return, planning constraints or downside risk.

02

Models sit apart from evidence

A spreadsheet can calculate an input, but it rarely verifies the rent, area, EPC, comparables or policy assumptions behind it.

03

Risk arrives too late

Location restrictions, regulation and auction-pack obligations are often discovered after time and professional fees have already been committed.

04

Decisions are hard to explain

Scores without sources or visible assumptions are difficult to trust, compare, share or defend to an investor, client or partner.

ONE CONNECTED DEAL RECORD
Listing or manual brief→Financial model→Local + policy evidence→Legal + physical risk→Decision + report

BoaTenure closes the gap by keeping the opportunity, assumptions, evidence and decision together throughout the workflow.

WHY NOW

The decision is becoming harder while the workflow remains fragmented.

Margins are tighterFinancing, tax and operating assumptions matter more when there is less room for error. Regulation keeps movingEPC requirements, tenancy reform, licensing and planning can change viability. Useful data is availablePublic and commercial sources can now be joined around a specific property decision. AI can connect the workApplied carefully, AI can interpret documents and context without replacing deterministic finance.

THE OUTPUT

One decision that can be inspected and communicated.

The listing was live when captured; availability and portal details can change. The £20,000 works budget, £190,000 after-works value and £1,150 rent are modelling assumptions, not seller claims or a promise that the property should be purchased. BoaTenure keeps those assumptions separate from imported evidence so they can be challenged, shared and revised.
